Atlas Legis

Card Export Bookmarklet

Drag the button below to your bookmarks bar — then click it on any school profile page to download each card as a PNG.

Step 1 — Drag this to your bookmarks bar
Export Cards as PNG

Drag the green button up into your browser's bookmarks bar. If the bar isn't visible, press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) to show it. Once saved, you only need to do this once.

2
Navigate to any school profile page
Open a school page on Atlas Legis — e.g. Cornell, Stanford, Yale. The page must be fully loaded.
3
Click "Export Cards as PNG" in your bookmarks bar
The bookmarklet loads a rendering library in the background (first use only takes a second), then silently captures each card and triggers a download for each one.
4
Check your Downloads folder
Files are named automatically — e.g. cornell-law-school_admissions-profile.png, cornell-law-school_employment-outcomes.png. Drop them straight into PowerPoint via Insert → Pictures.
Things to know
  • Browser compatibility: Works in Chrome, Edge, and Firefox. Safari may block automatic downloads after the first file — click Allow if prompted.
  • Downloads stagger 400ms apart to avoid the browser suppressing them as a spam flood. A page with 7 cards takes about 3 seconds total.
  • Font rendering: Output uses system serif/sans-serif fallbacks instead of the site's Google Fonts. Layout, colors, and spacing are pixel-perfect — only the typeface may differ slightly.
  • Resolution: Cards are rendered at 2× pixel density — sharp on high-DPI screens and projectors.
  • No internet required after first use on a given browser session — the rendering library is cached once loaded.
